Privacy Policy

Plain terms for a phone-first plumbing website.

What this site collects

This website has no forms, accounts or comment boxes, so we do not collect names, addresses or messages through it. Like any web server, the hosting service may log technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, which it uses to deliver and secure the site.

Calls and email

When you call (732) 466-1098 or email contact@247plumberneptune.com, we use what you tell us to answer your question, schedule work and give you a price. We do not sell your information. We keep job records for as long as we need them to service the work and meet legal and tax requirements.

Third-party content

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and, near the footer, a Google Maps embed. Those services may receive your IP address and browser details when the content loads, under their own privacy policies. We do not add advertising or analytics scripts of our own to this site.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own. Embedded third-party content may set cookies according to its provider's policy, and you can block them in your browser settings.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you or to ask us to delete it, email contact@247plumberneptune.com. We respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Changes

We may update this policy when our practices change. The date at the bottom of the page shows the latest revision.
