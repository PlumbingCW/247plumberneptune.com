Shark River Hills was marketed as a summer resort when it opened in 1923, and today it is a year-round Neptune Township neighborhood overlooking the Shark River. Homes here sit close to the water, and plumbing problems often start with water arriving from outside rather than failing inside.

This page covers flooded basements, sump pumps, sewer backups and storm recovery for Shark River Hills. Local facts come from the township's neighborhood page and the state's 2014 recovery report on Neptune Township.

What Superstorm Sandy showed about this neighborhood

The state's recovery report named Shark River Hills, Ocean Grove and North Island as the most damaged and most flood-susceptible neighborhoods in the township. In Shark River Hills, 8 residential structures were judged unsafe and 78 uninhabitable, and the surge isolated parts of the area from emergency services. Private damage across the township exceeded $10 million.

For a homeowner, the lesson is practical. Know your flood zone, keep power equipment above the likely waterline and have a plan for a night when roads are cut off. A sump pump with a battery backup keeps working through the power loss that often comes with a storm.

If water is coming into the lower level

Safety comes before saving belongings.

  1. Stay out of wet electrical areas

    Do not touch the panel or outlets while standing in water. Ask the utility or an electrician to cut power from outside if the panel is at risk.

  2. Stop the source you can control

    Close the main water valve if a pipe is leaking. Floodwater coming from outside cannot be shut off, so move to higher ground.

  3. Photograph everything

    Take photos and video of the water line and damaged items for your insurer before you move anything.

  4. Call us about the plumbing side

    Water heaters, boilers, sump pits and cleanouts that sat in water need inspection before restart.

A battery backup matters in any of the first three cases, since storms often cut power.

Sewer backups and the pump stations

The recovery report noted that three township pump stations were damaged in Sandy and later made watertight, with electronics raised above the 500-year flood elevation. It also warned that damaged stations risked raw sewage backing up into flooded streets. If sewage comes up a floor drain during a storm, the cause may be the public system, your lateral or both.

Stop using water and drains, and keep children and pets out of the area. A camera run through your cleanout shows whether your own line is clear, which tells you where to look next. Our sewer line cleaning page explains that process.

Building or renovating near the river

The township requires a Floodplain Development Permit before construction or development begins, and flood zone categories on its maps run from AE to X. A kitchen or bathroom remodel in a flood zone can raise questions that a plain swap does not. Ask the Neptune Township construction office what applies, and confirm current fees, before you order fixtures or a new water heater.
