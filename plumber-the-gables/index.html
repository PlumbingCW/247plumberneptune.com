Trees on the block, roots in the line

The Gables has wide, tree-lined streets, and the township notes residents planted 17 new trees in Gables Park on one Arbor Day. Mature and new trees are an asset, but their roots seek water, and a sewer lateral joint is a source. Roots can enter a small opening and slowly catch paper and grease until the line clogs.

If drains slow in more than one room, a camera inspection shows whether roots are the cause. Sewer line inspection gives a picture to base a decision on. If a root mass is the only problem, sewer line cleaning clears the line. If the pipe is broken, ask about sewer line repair.

Signs your lateral needs attention

These symptoms point to the line leaving the house rather than a single fixture.

Slow tub and floor drains

The lowest drain in the house shows a blockage first.

Gurgling toilets

Air displaced by a restriction bubbles back through the nearest trap.

Sewer smell indoors

A dry trap, cracked pipe or blockage can let sewer odor into the house.

Green patch over the line

A strip of unusually lush grass can mean a leak underground.

Water heater planning for a busy household

The township says The Gables is one of the most populated locations in Neptune, and busy households put steady demand on hot water. A tank that runs out during a normal morning may be too small, or may have sediment or a failed element. Our water heater replacement page explains how to size a new unit and what affects the price.

New Jersey's plumbing subcode is the National Standard Plumbing Code, and hot water under it is defined as 120 to 140 degrees F. Permit rules vary, so ask the Neptune Township construction office what a swap needs. The township's 2018 fee schedule lists $27 per fixture or device up to 99 gallons and $65 for 100 gallons and up. Confirm current fees.

Check any plumber's license yourself

Plumbers in New Jersey are licensed by the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, part of the Division of Consumer Affairs. The Division runs an online License Verification System. Ask any plumber for a license number and confirm it before work starts, including when you call us.
