About Neptune 24/7 Plumbing

A plumbing service built around one phone number and one promise: a clear price before work starts.

24/7 Emergency Line Open(732) 466-1098Call now!

Who we are

Neptune 24/7 Plumbing serves Neptune Township and the towns around it from 3297 Route 66 in Neptune, NJ. We take calls around the clock because plumbing problems rarely wait for business hours.

We are licensed and insured, and we handle residential and commercial work. We do not publish owner names, license numbers or customer counts on this site because we would rather give you facts you can check yourself than numbers you have to take on trust.

How a call works

Everything starts with the phone. This site has no booking forms, so you call (732) 466-1098 and describe the problem. We ask what you see, hear and smell, tell you what to shut off, and explain what a visit would involve.

On site, we diagnose before we recommend a repair. Depending on the job that can mean a drain camera, a pressure test, a meter check or a moisture reading. Then you get a clear price before work starts.

What we handle and what we do not

We cover drains, sewer lines, water heaters, leaks, burst pipes, toilets, faucets, sump pumps, repiping and commercial plumbing. See the full list of plumbing services.

Some problems are not plumbing. Foundation cracks, electrical faults and oil burner service belong to other trades, and we say so plainly when your problem falls outside our work. A straight answer saves you a wasted visit.

Licensing and permits

Plumbers in New Jersey are licensed by the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, part of the Division of Consumer Affairs. The Division runs an online License Verification System, and we encourage you to use it on any plumber you hire.

The state plumbing subcode is the National Standard Plumbing Code, in its New Jersey edition. Permit rules vary by job and town, so ask the Neptune Township construction office or your own town's office before work begins. We flag it when a job is likely to need a permit and inspection.

What we check on a visit

A diagnostic visit starts with questions and a look at the fixture, then moves to tools only where the symptom calls for them. A slow drain may get a camera. A high water bill may get a meter test with every fixture off. A water heater that runs out of hot water may get its thermostat, elements or burner, anode rod and sediment checked.

We explain what we find in plain words and show you where we can. Then we give you the price for the repair that fits, so you are choosing between real options instead of guessing.

Who we work for

Most calls come from homeowners in single-family houses, condos and older shore cottages. We also work with landlords and property managers who need a problem explained and priced without being on site, and with small businesses along Route 66 and the shore towns.

Seasonal and second homes are common near the water. Census figures list about a third of the housing units in Bradley Beach as vacant and close to 30 percent in Belmar, which is why a shutoff check before a house sits empty for months is worth doing.

What we will not promise

We do not promise an arrival time, because it depends on the job in front of us, the roads and the weather. We tell you what to do while you wait and what the visit will involve.

We do not quote a major repair before we have seen the problem, since a drain that looks like a clog can be a broken line and a leak under a slab can start somewhere else. The price comes after the diagnosis and before the work.

Questions people ask before calling

Many people want to know whether a problem can wait until morning. If water is running that you cannot stop, sewage is backing up, or a water heater is leaking, call now. If a faucet drips or a drain is slow but still draining, you can usually plan a visit, and we will say which category it sounds like.

People also ask whether we charge for coming out. We give you a clear price before work starts, so ask about the visit when you call and we will explain how it works for your situation.

Where we work

Our home area is Neptune Township, including Ocean Grove and Shark River Hills. We also cover Neptune City, Asbury Park, Ocean Township, Bradley Beach and Belmar. The service area page has the details for each town.

If you are just outside those towns, call and ask. We would rather tell you honestly whether we can help than leave you guessing.

How to reach us

Call (732) 466-1098 at any hour. For non-urgent questions you can email contact@247plumberneptune.com. The contact page has our address and a map.
