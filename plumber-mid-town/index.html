Mid-Town is the Neptune Township neighborhood bounded by Asbury Avenue on the north, Neptune Boulevard on the west, Corlies Avenue (Route 33) on the south and Memorial Drive and the Asbury Park border on the east. The township describes the early 1960s housing as starter houses and apartments, and the area has both owner-occupied homes and rentals today.

That mix drives most of the calls here: a tenant who needs to know whom to notify, an owner weighing a repair against a replacement, and a shop owner on West Lake Avenue with a drain that will not clear. This page explains each situation and where to read more.

Renting in Mid-Town: what to do when plumbing fails

Tenants and landlords both have a stake in a fast, documented response.

  1. Shut off what you can

    Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff if water is running. Know where the main valve is before an emergency.

  2. Notify the landlord in writing

    Send a text or email with the date, the problem and photos. A written record helps if there is damage or a dispute.

  3. Do not hire without permission

    Many leases require the landlord to approve repairs. Ask before you pay for work.

  4. Call the 24/7 line for advice

    Call (732) 466-1098 and we will explain what is urgent and what can wait. Landlords and property managers can ask for a price over the phone.

West Lake Avenue businesses

The township says West Lake Avenue was long the area's commercial strip, with restaurants, pharmacies, barber shops and other businesses. The same kinds of shops need drain, hot water and fixture work today, usually scheduled around opening hours. Kitchens need grease handled properly, and salons and barber shops need steady hot water and clear floor drains.

Our commercial plumbing page explains how we approach a shop or small office, including what to tell a landlord who owns the building.

A neighborhood still being renewed

In 1997 Mid-Town residents and the Township Committee formed the Midtown Neighborhood Empowerment Council and partnered with the State of New Jersey to begin revitalization. If you plan a renovation or are buying a property, confirm with the Neptune Township construction office what permits apply. Permit rules vary, and the 2018 fee schedule lists $27 per plumbing fixture or device and a $95 minimum per subcode. Confirm current fees.

Before the plumber arrives at your Mid-Town address

These steps limit damage and speed the visit.

  • Close the shutoff valve for the affected fixture.
  • Move belongings away from the wet area.
  • Take photos or video of the damage.
  • Tell the landlord or property manager.
  • Clear a path to the main valve, water heater and cleanout.
  • Stay away from outlets and panels near standing water.
